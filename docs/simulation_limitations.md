# Simulation Limitations

- Results are simulated counterfactual estimates, not observed facts or guarantees.
- Causal effects are not established.
- Capacity is `CAPACITY_NOT_AVAILABLE` unless supplied in the state.
- Demand redistribution and risk recalculation are `NOT_ESTIMATED` without the corresponding engines and data.
- Uncertainty is `UNCERTAINTY_NOT_ESTIMATED` in the deterministic core.
- Synthetic roads are labeled `HYPOTHETICAL_INFRASTRUCTURE`.
