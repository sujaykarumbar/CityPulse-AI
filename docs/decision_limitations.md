# Decision Limitations

- Pareto solutions represent trade-offs, not universal winners.
- Objective weights must be supplied by the analyst.
- Missing cost data prevents valid cost optimization.
- Simulation and forecast errors propagate into decision results.
- Causal effects are not established automatically.
- Human judgment remains necessary.
- Unavailable candidate metrics are not fabricated; candidates are marked `not_available` or `not_run`.
